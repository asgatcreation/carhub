Seller questions

How much does it cost to list?

Listing is free. CarHub earns a small commission only when a buyer completes a reservation.

Why is my listing "pending review"?

New sellers' listings are checked by our team to keep the marketplace free of scams and duplicate ads. Getting verified as a dealer removes this step.

How do deposits work?

The buyer pays a ₦250,000 refundable deposit to reserve your car. The car is hidden from other buyers while they arrange an inspection, then they pay you the balance directly.

Can I edit or remove my listing?

Yes — manage everything from My listings, including marking the car as sold.
