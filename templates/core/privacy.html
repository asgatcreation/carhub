This policy explains what personal data CarHub collects, why, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number and password (stored as a secure hash). If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email and profile picture from Google, nothing else.
  • Orders and bookings: delivery or pickup address, inspection preferences, items bought, trip pickup and drop-off points, fares and ratings.
  • Location: only when you choose "use my location" when booking a ride, and, for drivers, while they are online in the driver app so riders can track their trip. You can turn it off at any time in your browser.
  • Verification documents from dealers, vendors and drivers (ID, CAC or licence). These are seen only by our moderation team and never shown publicly.
  • Messages you send to sellers through CarHub chat, kept so either side can refer back to them and so we can investigate reports.

How we use it

  • To run your account, reservations, orders and trips, and to send codes, receipts and status updates by email.
  • To verify sellers, vendors and drivers and keep fraud off the platform.
  • To show you parts that fit your car and prices compared with the market.

We don't sell your data and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The other party to your transaction: a seller sees the name and phone number of a buyer who reserved their car; a vendor sees the delivery details for their order; a driver sees the rider's first name, pickup point and phone number during a trip.
  • Service providers that run parts of CarHub: Paystack (payments), Brevo (email delivery), Google (sign-in), and our hosting provider. Maps use OpenStreetMap, Photon and OSRM; the addresses you search for are sent to those services to find and route them.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in, protect forms (CSRF) and remember your cart and theme. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is open. Order, trip and payment records are kept for up to 6 years for accounting and dispute purposes. Verification documents are deleted 12 months after a decision unless you remain an active seller, vendor or driver.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your personal data, object to its use, or ask for a copy. Update most details in Account settings, or contact us for anything else. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Passwords are hashed, the site is served over HTTPS, sign-in and password resets use one-time codes with rate limits, and staff access is restricted to the moderation team.
