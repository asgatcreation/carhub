1. Who we are

CarHub is an online marketplace that connects people buying and selling cars, vendors selling parts and accessories, and riders with independent drivers in Nigeria. CarHub is not the seller of cars or parts and does not employ drivers; we provide the platform, verification and payment tools.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 and give accurate details. Verify your email with the code we send you.
  • You can sign in with email and password or with Google; both lead to the same account if they share an email.
  • Keep your password and codes private. We never ask for them by phone, chat or email.

3. Buying a car

  • Reserving a car takes a refundable ₦250,000 deposit per car, which takes the car off the market while you arrange an inspection.
  • The deposit is refunded in full if you cancel before the sale completes, if the seller cancels, or if the car is not as described. You pay the balance directly to the seller after inspecting the car.
  • Inspect the car, its papers and history yourself or with a mechanic before paying the balance.

4. Selling a car or parts

  • Listings must describe the item honestly with real photos. New sellers' listings, all vendor products and all reviews are checked by our team before they appear.
  • Verified dealers and vendors must keep their ID or CAC documents current. We may remove listings or suspend accounts that mislead buyers.
  • Vendors must ship orders within the stated dispatch time, mark them shipped with a courier, and refund cancelled items.

5. Parts orders

  • Prices include VAT where applicable; delivery is charged by state and is free over ₦100,000. Pay on delivery is offered in Lagos and Abuja up to ₦500,000.
  • You can cancel any item before it ships for a full refund. Check the compatible-cars list and part number before ordering.

6. Rides and chauffeurs

  • Fares shown before booking are estimates based on the planned route and typical traffic; busy-hour pricing may apply and is shown before you book.
  • Cancelling is free until your driver arrives; after that a ₦500 late-cancellation fee applies.
  • For chauffeur bookings you remain responsible for your car, its insurance, fuel and tolls. Bookings are for a minimum of 3 hours.
  • Drivers must hold a valid licence, which we verify, and must share their location while on a trip so riders can track them.

7. Payments

Online payments are processed by Paystack. CarHub never sees or stores your card details. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

8. Reviews and conduct

Reviews must be about a real experience. Don't post contact details, advertising, abuse or anything unlawful. We may reject or remove content that breaks these rules.

9. Liability

CarHub verifies sellers, vendors and drivers and moderates listings, but the contract for a car, part or ride is between you and the other party. To the extent allowed by law, CarHub is not liable for indirect losses arising from those transactions.

We may update these terms; we'll show the date of the latest change at the top of this page. These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
