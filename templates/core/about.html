About CarHub

Making car buying in Nigeria honest.

Buying a car shouldn't mean endless calls, wasted trips and fake listings. CarHub brings verified sellers, transparent pricing and protected reservations into one place.

Our mission

Trust at every step of the journey

We review every listing before it goes live, verify dealers' identity and business documents, and hold buyers' deposits until they've inspected the car. Sellers get serious buyers; buyers get peace of mind.

Our vision is a marketplace where anyone in Nigeria can buy or sell a car online as confidently as they would at a trusted dealer down the road.

About this project

CarHub is a portfolio project built with Django 5, Channels (WebSockets), django-allauth and Paystack. The listings, dealers and reviews are demo data; vehicle photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are credited on each listing under their Creative Commons licences.
